# Product Roadmap

This roadmap is directional, not a fixed delivery schedule.

# Near Term

- stabilize extraction quality
- mature Hero Card as the first Opportunity Engine consumer
- introduce early planner flows
- improve CTA usefulness and freshness

# Mid Term

- create a real planner experience
- separate personalization from Hero Card heuristics
- build recommendation retrieval and ranking
- strengthen discovery as more than saved-place browsing

# Longer Term

- context-aware opportunities
- AI-assisted ranking
- predictive trip planning
- collaborative planning and stronger social surfaces
