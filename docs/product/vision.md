# Wandreel Vision

Wandreel should help users move from passive travel inspiration to active travel decisions.

The core experience is:

- capture inspiration from reels, links, and content
- turn that inspiration into structured places
- help users organize what matters
- surface the next best action
- eventually help users plan and execute real trips

Wandreel should grow from a save-and-sort product into an intelligent travel decision platform.

# Product Principles

- Start from the user’s real saved intent.
- Reduce clutter by surfacing the most useful next action.
- Prefer structured, explainable systems before opaque ones.
- Let AI enhance good product architecture rather than replace it.
